## Human Rights and Political Non-profit Memberships

### Future Russia - Switzerland

[Future Russia - Switzerland](https://www.futurerussia.ch/en) is an active association that brings together Russians and Russian-speaking residents of Switzerland.
The goal is to promote and apply democratic principles, protect human rights, and develop civil society in Russia.
The organization supports and organizes various events in Switzerland and abroad.

Oleg's roles: Community and Social Media Management; Infrastructure and IT team member.

### Peaceful Lights 2025

[Peaceful Lights](https://ogonki.tv/) is a New Year's Eve musical project, produced by volunteers.
Russian liberal opposition activists created it as an alternative to the New Year's shows on Russian television.

Oleg's roles: IT team & community adviser.
